# RNNoise models (FFmpeg `arnndn`)

- `sh.rnnn` — "somnolent-hogwash": speech in recorded noise (used by *Voice*, and by *Strong*
  with an extra FFT pass). On noisy speech it removed more noise than the general-purpose
  "conjoined-burgers" model, which is therefore not shipped.

Source: https://github.com/richardpl/arnndn-models (converted from
https://github.com/GregorR/rnnoise-models). Per the original README, the trained models are not
creative work and not subject to copyright. Fetched 2026-10-30.
